01 / TERMS

Terms of service

MortalShade Network offers digital Minecraft server perks such as ranks, keys and virtual coins. These are digital items for use within the relevant MortalShade game mode; they are not cash, deposits or investments and have no cash redemption value unless a mandatory law says otherwise.

  • Check the game mode, item description, price, coin amount and duration before placing an order.
  • Enter the correct Minecraft username. An incorrect username can delay or prevent delivery; contact support promptly if you notice an error.
  • Do not exploit bugs, abuse promotions, reverse-engineer the store or use fraudulent payment methods.
  • Ranks, permissions and item functionality may change for security, balancing or server maintenance. We should communicate material changes to affected players.
  • Minecraft and Mojang/Microsoft are not affiliated with or endorsing this store. MortalShade is an independent server network.

These terms do not remove consumer rights that cannot legally be excluded. If a mandatory local law conflicts with these terms, that law takes priority.

02 / REFUNDS

Refund & cancellation policy

Because ranks, keys and coins are digital goods, please review your order carefully before paying. This draft policy does not claim that all digital purchases are automatically non-refundable.

  • If a payment is charged but the purchase is not delivered, contact support with the payment reference and Minecraft username so the order can be checked.
  • Duplicate charges, verified payment errors, failed delivery and incorrect fulfilment will be investigated.
  • Refunds or replacements will be handled according to applicable consumer law, the payment provider's rules and the facts of the order.
  • Where a refund is approved or legally required, it will be sent through the original payment method where possible. The payment provider or bank may control the time it takes to appear.
  • Do not send a second payment while a transaction is pending. Contact support first.

Before real payments are enabled: the operator must set and publish a clear refund decision process, support response target and refund processing target that match applicable law and the chosen payment provider.

03 / DELIVERY

Digital delivery policy

Products are intended for the game mode shown on each listing. The planned delivery system will route LifeSteal purchases to LifeSteal, Survival purchases to Survival and other products to their configured destination.

  • Delivery should begin only after the payment provider confirms payment to the server backend.
  • If a player is offline, the planned system will keep the item queued until that player can receive it.
  • Players should check that their username and selected product are correct before paying.
  • Maintenance, account-name issues or server outages can delay fulfilment. The live store must show a realistic delivery estimate before checkout.

Current status: automatic in-game delivery and live payment processing are not active on this preview website. Do not send money manually.

04 / PRIVACY

Privacy notice

This preview stores the shopping cart and age-confirmation flag in your browser's local storage. The demo checkout does not submit an order or process a payment.

When a live store is launched, the operator may need to process a Minecraft username, order details, payment status, support messages and limited technical/security data to fulfil purchases, prevent fraud and respond to requests. Payment card or UPI credentials should be handled by the payment provider, not stored by this website.

  • Only collect information needed for order fulfilment, security, legal compliance and customer support.
  • Publish the identity and contact details of the data controller/operator, the purposes and legal basis for processing, retention periods, processors, user rights and complaint process before collecting real customer data.
  • Do not enter passwords, OTPs, UPI PINs or payment-card details into a support message or Minecraft username field.
  • Browser storage can be cleared through your browser settings; doing so may remove your saved cart and age-confirmation preference.

This is a starter notice, not a final jurisdiction-specific privacy policy. The operator must review it against the laws that apply to the business and its customers, including India's data-protection requirements where applicable.
